Survival World — development journal
2026-11-25: inspected initial 8 KB Three.js prototype; it only had basic movement, collection, and a placeholder attack. Replaced with an expanded single-file playable prototype.
Implemented in this iteration: 3D third-person scene; desktop keyboard and touch joystick/buttons; resource nodes; zombie chase/attack and player melee; axe craft; hunger and food; bandage craft/use; wall/door/storage/fire craft and placement; mission progression and XP/levels; day/night lighting; local save/load; timed survival race, resource contest and practice duel; resize handling and reduced pixel ratio for mobile.
Verification pending: JavaScript parse/static checks and browser runtime checks. This build is single-player only. Storage is currently represented by placed crates, not an interactive inventory container; door placement is decorative; leaderboard/tournament persistence and authoritative multiplayer are not implemented. Real multiplayer needs server-side state, authentication, networking and anti-cheat validation.
